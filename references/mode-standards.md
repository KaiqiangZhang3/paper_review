# Mode Standards

Use the intake answer to select the closest mode. If the submitted work does not fit one mode cleanly, state the chosen mode and the reason.

## Master's Paper

Purpose: assess whether the paper demonstrates sound legal training and a workable scholarly argument at the master's level.

Emphasize:

- A clear research question that can be answered within the submitted scope.
- A central claim that is identifiable, contestable, and supported.
- Basic command of relevant international law sources.
- Accurate use of treaties, customary international law, cases, state practice, opinio juris, soft law, institutional materials, and secondary literature where relevant.
- A structure that moves the reader from problem to legal framework to analysis.
- Paragraph-level clarity and disciplined use of footnotes.
- Evidence that the author understands course expectations when a course rubric is supplied.

Common serious defects:

- Descriptive topic survey without an argument.
- Confusing policy preference with legal analysis.
- Treating soft law, institutional reports, cases, and treaty rules as interchangeable authority.
- Citing international law materials for broad propositions they do not support.
- Sections that summarize sources without integrating them into the thesis.

## Doctoral Paper

Purpose: assess whether the paper can support advanced scholarly contribution.

Emphasize:

- **Problem scope and scale**: Can the thesis clearly articulate what problem it addresses and at what level of significance? Are boundaries defined?
- **Methodological path selection**: Does the thesis justify its chosen analytical framework and acknowledge alternative approaches? Does it explain why this path is appropriate?
- **Contribution and challenge**: Does the thesis explicitly state what it contributes to or challenges in existing scholarship? Is this contribution demonstrated rather than asserted?
- **Adverse scholarship engagement**: Does the thesis identify contrary scholarly positions and address them substantively within analytical chapters (not just in a disconnected literature review)?
- Originality, significance, and precision of the research intervention.
- Explicit method or theory where the argument depends on one.
- Depth of literature engagement and accurate positioning against existing scholarship.
- Breadth and control of primary legal materials.
- Handling of contrary authority, adverse facts, alternative explanations, and methodological limits when that check is requested.
- Whether chapters or sections cumulatively advance a larger scholarly claim.
- Whether the writing distinguishes doctrinal analysis, normative argument, institutional analysis, and empirical claims.

Common serious defects:

- **Problem scope undefined or inflated**: The thesis states a problem so broadly that it cannot be meaningfully addressed, or never specifies boundaries.
- **Methodological silence**: The thesis adopts an analytical approach without explaining or justifying it; alternatives are not acknowledged.
- **Contribution by topic selection alone**: The thesis claims originality because it addresses an under-studied area but does not articulate what new understanding it generates.
- **Adverse scholarship ignored or cited without engagement**: Contrary positions appear in the literature review but are never addressed in analytical chapters.
- The thesis is broad but not original.
- Literature review is separated from the argument instead of shaping it.
- Legal materials are numerous but not controlled.
- The paper avoids the strongest objections to its claim.
- The contribution depends on a method the paper does not explain.

### Thesis-Specific Assessment for Doctoral Work

For doctoral dissertations and thesis chapters, apply the four-dimension framework from `thesis-assessment-framework.md`:

1. **Problem Scope and Scale** (多大的事儿) — Does the thesis clearly define the problem's nature, significance, and boundaries?
2. **Methodological Path and Alternatives** (脉络选择) — Does the thesis justify its chosen intellectual framework and acknowledge competing approaches?
3. **Contribution and Challenge** (贡献/挑战) — Does the thesis explicitly state what it contributes or challenges, and is this demonstrated?
4. **Adverse Scholarship Engagement** (学术挑战的处理) — Does the thesis identify and substantively respond to contrary scholarly positions?

Include a dedicated "Thesis-Specific Assessment" section in the review memorandum after standard dimensions.

## Peer-Review Manuscript

Purpose: assess publishability, contribution, and legal reliability.

Emphasize:

- Novelty and importance of the claim.
- Fit with the target field or journal if supplied.
- Relationship to existing literature and live debates.
- Soundness of legal characterization and source hierarchy.
- Whether the manuscript's defects are fundamental or reparable.
- Structural efficiency and whether the paper spends space where the contribution is strongest.
- Clarity of the abstract, introduction, roadmap, and conclusion.

Common serious defects:

- The claim is already familiar in the literature.
- The contribution is asserted but not demonstrated.
- The paper relies on controversial legal propositions without support.
- The structure buries the intervention.
- The article needs a different framing to reach its likely audience.

Do not default to an accept, revise, or reject recommendation. Provide that recommendation only if the professor asks for it.

## Other Texts

For abstracts, outlines, proposals, and chapters, adapt the closest mode while limiting the strength of conclusions. State what cannot be assessed from the submitted material.
